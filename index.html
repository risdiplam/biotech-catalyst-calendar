Evidence discipline

Methodology

A broad, source-linked sweep of disclosed 2027 binaries, with uncertainty shown rather than buried and known timing gaps kept visible.

HARDExact date from FDA or an event organizer.
GUIDANCECompany-guided window such as Q1 or H2.
INFERREDDerived from registry or submission timelines; highest slip risk.

Universe

The dataset contains 1,047 source-linked records across 284 tickers with qualifying clinical or regulatory events. Conference rows are excluded from the ticker-with-events count.

Universe hygiene: acquired, delisted, renamed, stale, and duplicate entries were removed or remapped across the full dataset.

Evidence order

SEC filings → FDA and regulatory records → ClinicalTrials.gov and History of Changes → conference programs → company PRs and decks → genuine wires and verbatim-source fallbacks.

PDUFA gate: an entry is labeled PDUFA only when an FDA acceptance letter or equivalent acceptance disclosure supports it. Submission-only and acceptance-contingent events stay regulatory watches.

What counts

Every clinical data readout regardless of stage; IND, NDA, and BLA filings and acceptances; PDUFAs; posted AdComs; other binary regulatory decisions; and major conferences with stock-relevant data. No date is invented: unguided timelines receive no entry.

Dataset shape

The dataset contains 1,047 records: 184 Phase 3, 333 Phase 2, 224 Phase 1, 8 preclinical, and 298 regulatory-or-conference records without a clinical phase. Event types include 720 readouts, 38 PDUFAs, 19 NDA filings, 17 BLA filings, 16 INDs, 37 other regulatory events, and 200 conferences.

Date quality is 230 hard, 317 guidance, and 500 inferred.

Known blind spots

  • 47.8% of rows (500/1,047) are inferred. ClinicalTrials.gov primary-completion dates do not equal — or guarantee — public topline disclosure.
  • There are zero hard Phase 3 readout dates in this dataset. Hard dates are organizer-confirmed conferences and FDA-grounded dates, including some PDUFAs; clinical readout timing is guidance or inference.
  • No 2027 AdComs are listed because FDA has not posted them yet.
  • H2 2027 PDUFAs remain incomplete until late-2026 FDA acceptances land. The monthly refresh on the 1st of each month is designed to catch them.
  • Every event carries a source link. Official company PR/IR, SEC filings, FDA, ClinicalTrials.gov, and canonical newswires are used wherever they exist. BioSpace, STAT, Endpoints News, similar established biotech outlets, and call transcripts hosted on Quartr are accepted as legitimate sources. Rows that remain on weaker aggregators, wire mirrors, or trade coverage are kept with confidence explicitly flagged in the evidence note; no row is removed for source quality alone.
  • Unguided programs remain on the watchlist rather than receiving invented dates.

v1.5 full audit

  • All 1,048 records were re-verified on September 20, 2026. Sixty audit findings were applied across regulatory, Phase 3, Phase 2, Phase 1/preclinical, conference, identity, timing, phase, and duplicate checks.
  • All 36 existing PDUFAs were confirmed against FDA or company sources. Two missing PDUFAs were then added: ZBIO obexelimab on May 27, 2027, and BMRN VOXZOGO (vosoritide) on February 28, 2027, bringing the calendar to 38 PDUFAs.
  • Official-source remediation covered company PR/IR, FDA, ClinicalTrials.gov, SEC, organizers, and canonical newswires. Every row includes an evidence link; accepted biotech news outlets and Quartr transcripts remain valid, while weaker fallbacks stay visibly flagged rather than being deleted for source reasons alone.
  • The audited date-quality mix is 500 inferred, 317 guidance, and 231 hard.
